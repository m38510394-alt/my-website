The Rat Mouse is a one of a kind computer mouse, no other can compare. Using a special gelatin-like compound dubbed ratatin, the mouse is able to maintain structural integrity while also being slightly malleable similar to real rats. Small heaters are placed strategically throughout the Rat Mouse, similar to the real warmth of life. This alongside the hyperelastic fur and skin come together to form a truly unique computer mouse.
